# Qualified review checklist

Use this document to organize a real review. A completed checklist is not a substitute for institutional review, validation, or regulatory assessment. The app must remain `pending_review` until a qualified reviewer documents an actual decision.

- Reviewer role and relevant qualifications:
- Review date:
- Scope reviewed:
- Sources or reference cases used:
- Decision: pending / approved for a stated educational scope / changes required / rejected
- Limitations or conditions on use:

## Anatomy and asset provenance

- Confirm every model's source, license, credit, and permission for the intended distribution.
- Check chamber, valve, great-vessel, coronary origin, branching, and orientation relationships against authoritative reference material.
- Confirm which structures are actual mesh geometry, which are texture detail, and which are simplified teaching overlays.
- Check the left/right coronary courses, branch labels, coronary dominance, and stated model limitations.
- Confirm that model animations and any pulse effect do not imply validated myocardial mechanics.

## Learning and safety content

- Confirm cases are synthetic and contain no identifiable patient data.
- Review case findings, answer keys, allergy notices, rationale, and uncertainty language.
- Confirm no control or score implies real procedural instruction or clinical competency.
- Check that simulated physiology and image appearance are clearly distinguished from measured clinical data.

## Projection and device visuals

- Compare projection orientation and vessel overlap against documented reference projections.
- Check that the C-arm pane is labelled as a geometric educational demo, not diagnostic fluoroscopy.
- Review guidewire route, target markers, and device proportions as conceptual visuals only.
- Define evidence required before adding collision mechanics, lesion response, radiation, or physiological claims.

## Accessibility and usability

- Keyboard operation, focus visibility, screen-reader labels, reduced motion, contrast, text size, and mobile layouts.
- Whether learners can distinguish the two model sources and understand how to switch views.
- Whether error and loading messages explain recovery.

## Sign-off discipline

Record reviewer findings in the project review process with versioned changes and sources. Do not enter patient-identifying information in the browser's local notes feature. Only an authorized maintainer should update machine-readable review status after reviewing the record.
